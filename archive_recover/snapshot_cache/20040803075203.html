You will need Acrobat Reader for the above PDF files.
 

The AWTA (American Working Terrier Association) is not affiliated with JRTCA, AKC, or UKC and welcomes many terrier breeds and dachshunds to participate in its program.  You will find it similar to our Go To Ground; in fact, anyone who has competed in Go To Ground at our trial should feel comfortable with their tests. The AWTA issues Certificates of Gameness (CG) to dogs qualifying at their trials in the Open Division, Hunting Certificates (HC) to dogs used regularly for hunting over a period of a year, and Working Certificates (WC) to dogs which qualify with work in a natural den.

Our judge will be Russ Wischover.

Registration table opens at 8:00 AM

Competition begins at 9:00 AM

Although this date was originally a Fun Day, we will not offer GTG this day and we will race only if and when the AWTA den trial is completed. Come and participate in the den trial; you neednt be a member of the AWTA to participate; schooling will be available after the completion of the Novice work.
