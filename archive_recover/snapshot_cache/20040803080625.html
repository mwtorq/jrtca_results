Adult Racing Championship for 12 - 15 terriers

Larry Schindler and his terrier, Jackwalla Rambo, were the first recipients of the Prestige Farms Oliver perpetual trophy, sponsored by Jennifer Gilligan and Steve Geroux. Larry received a keeper momento and Rambos name will be the first to be engraved on the beautiful plate. If you didnt get a chance to see the plate its very unique and features a photo of that good lookin terrier, Oliver.

Bronze Medallion

Thanks to Charlene Schlueter for organizing our very special Bronze Medallion classit was wonderful! Each terrier earning their Bronze Medallion since last years trial received a chair and a Merit in the Field ribbon commemorating the day. Two older, well-seasoned Medallion holders were honored: Button Top Thistle, owned by Susie Parsons, and Wild Goose Kerm, owned by Charlene.
