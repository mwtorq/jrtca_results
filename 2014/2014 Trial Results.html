Results from JRTCA Sanctioned Trials

  

 

  

Welcome
In this section of the site you will find trial results as well as the Gallery of Champions and photos for terriers who have earned their Bronze Medallion for 2014.
We have also added a new "Gallery of Challenge Awards" for the winners of 2012-2013 Challenge Awards.
Below listed by date are the JRTCA Sanctioned Trial Results that have been submitted by the Trial Chairs. The Chairs have 60 days post trial to submit their results. If you find errors in the results please contact THE TRIAL CHAIRPERSON not the JRTCA to have the results corrected. This includes typos, wrong results, missing classes, etc. Click on the Trial Name to view a separate page for each trial. 

NOTE: You will need Adobe Acrobat to view and print certain pages on this website.
*************
IMPORTANT NOTICE
PHOTOS FOR JRTCA PUBLICATIONS
Photos for any JRTCA publication (Yearbook, True Grit, etc.) will be kept on file for THREE (3) YEARS ONLY. If your terrier's photo is older than three (3) years, and still being published, please submit a new photo as soon as possible.
Only ONE (1) photo per terrier is kept on file. When a new photo is submitted, it replaces the one previously on file.
REMINDER - ALL Champions at sanctioned trials, for all divisions, are now being posted online.

The following is a list of trial results...as new results are received we will update this website accordingly. Instead of having a separate picture section, the photos of winners will be available for each trial in the Gallery of Champions by the terrier's name, sorted alphabetically.
